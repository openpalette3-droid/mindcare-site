Care that fits your life

Therapy, groups, daily check-ins and a toll-free line, in Kinyarwanda and English.

What you can use MindCare for

Video and voice sessions

One-to-one sessions of 45–60 minutes for anxiety, depression, stress, trauma and relationships.

Group sessions

Small groups of up to 5, led by a therapist, for people who want to learn from each other.

Daily check-in

A chatbot in Kinyarwanda and English asks how you've been and connects you to a therapist when you need one.

Free guides

Articles, videos and self-help tools about mental health, made for Rwanda.

Toll-free line

Talk to a trained care officer if you have no internet, or if things feel urgent.

For workplaces and schools

Wellness plans for organisations at 4,000 RWF per session.
