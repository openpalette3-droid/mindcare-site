Hospital patients get care closer to home

MindCare works with public hospitals in every province. A kiosk at the hospital lets you see a MindCare therapist without travelling to Kigali, and you can continue from your phone afterwards.

How linking your hospital works

1. Create your account

Choose “a patient at a partner hospital” when you sign up.

2. Add your details

Enter your hospital and your patient number.

3. You stay in control

Your hospital confirms the link, and you choose what your care team can see.
