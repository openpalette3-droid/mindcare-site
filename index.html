Help should be easy to find, and easy to ask for

In our survey of more than 100 Rwandans, 95% said they would use an online platform for mental health care. The biggest reasons people had never sought help were not knowing where to go, not knowing it existed, and fear of being judged.

Know where to go

One place for therapy, free guides and a toll-free line, in the languages people speak at home.

Private by design

Privacy mattered most to people we surveyed. Sessions are confidential and you can sign up with a nickname.

Priced for real life

Student rates, Mutuelle co-pays and group sessions keep care within reach.
